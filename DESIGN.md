## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the tension between high-fashion editorial rigor and authoritative machine intelligence. Rather than feeling like a transactional e-commerce discount engine, the experience mirrors a bespoke personal stylist and quantitative wardrobe advisor. It replaces impulse-driven uncertainty with calm, data-backed certainty.

The aesthetic combines **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Modernism**:
- **Editorial Typography & Space:** High-contrast dramatic serif headers establish prestige, authority, and curation, evoking modern runway lookbooks and international broadsheets.
- **Rational Machine UI:** Balanced by disciplined, geometric sans-serif data visualizations, confidence dials, and contextual decision panels.
- **Restraint Over Flash:** High-density discount badges and aggressive sales counters are omitted. Value is expressed through architectural clarity, generous whitespace, pristine micro-borders, and tactile card surfaces.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires reassurance, aesthetic elevation, and composed confidence—transforming choice paralysis into decisive wardrobe curation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ivory-lit atelier backdrop punctuated by high-contrast charcoal typography, precise status indicators, and an intentional magenta accent.

### Color Tokens & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBF9`):** A warm, gallery-grade off-white that reduces glare and frames garments with museum-level neutrality.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp white reserved for primary interactive cards, floating drawer panels, and modal layers.
- **Surface Border (`#E8E8E5`):** Ultra-fine neutral hairline outline to anchor cards without visual weight.
- **Primary Ink (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep carbon charcoal providing assertive contrast for headlines, values, and primary buttons.
- **Secondary Ink (`#6E6E6C`):** Subdued mineral slate for metadata, rationale notes, and supporting context.
- **Brand Accent (`#E11B6C`):** Distinctive fashion magenta utilized strictly for active copilot moments, curated highlights, and brand identity. Hover state transitions to `#C7145D`, while `#FDF0F5` serves as an interactive wash.

### Decision & Confidence Spectrum
Machine intelligence feedback is codified into three precise tonal tiers:
- **High Confidence / Buy Now:** Emerald ink (`#059669`), wash (`#ECFDF5`), hairline (`#A7F3D0`). Signals perfect fit harmony and optimal price stability.
- **Medium Confidence / Monitor:** Warm amber ink (`#D97706`), wash (`#FEF3C7`), hairline (`#FDE68A`). Signals impending price trend shifts or minor sizing nuances.
- **High Risk / Fit Doubts:** Coral crimson ink (`#DC2626`), wash (`#FEF2F2`), hairline (`#FECACA`). Flags silhouette mismatches, high return-rate items, or fluctuating inventory.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast editorial serifs of **Bodoni Moda** with the clean geometric balance of **Outfit**.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Bodoni Moda`):** Utilized exclusively for screen titles, high-level collection headings, and hero copilot affirmations. Creates an immediate tone of luxury curation.
- **Intelligence Sans (`Outfit`):** Drives all operational UI—product attributes, copilot rationales, metrics, status pills, and interactive inputs. Its open geometric aperture ensures legibility at micro scales.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic couture label typography and distinguish system parameters from editorial prose.
- **Numerics:** Wardrobe match percentages, sizing scores, and pricing trends utilize medium and bold Outfit cuts with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The copilot operates on an 8pt architectural rhythm, structured around a mobile-first philosophy that translates into a focused, framed atelier cockpit on wider viewports.

### Viewport Framing & Grid Structure
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 480px):** Single-column fluid stack with `1rem` edge gutters. Bottom navigation bars and floating decision drawers stay fixed within thumb reach.
- **Tablet / Split View (481px – 1023px):** 6-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters. The visual item card pairs side-by-side with the copilot intelligence module.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Employs a centered device shell (`440px` maximum content width) positioned against a wide, ambient desktop canvas (`1280px`). This presentation focuses decision-making, framing the wishlist as a dedicated handheld intelligence device with auxiliary contextual telemetry (lookbook reference, wardrobe overlap analysis) living in wider ambient side rails.

### Spacing Cadence
- In-card elements (score pills, fit analysis lines) use compact `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` vertical gaps.
- Structural transitions between product assets and copilot insight modules require `1.5rem`.
- Section divides maintain `3rem` to ensure an airy, unhurried reading pace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through structural layering, crisp hairline boundaries, and translucent surface blurs rather than heavy shadows.

### Surface System
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FBFBF9`. Flat base layer representing the atelier desk.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces defined by a continuous `1px` border of `#E8E8E5`. Shadows are omitted or kept microscopic (`0 1px 2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.03)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Drawer & Popover):** `#FFFFFF` floating panels with a diffused ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`. Retains the sharp `1px` border in `#E8E8E5`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Critical Overlay):** Backed by a high-fashion scrim: `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.4)` with an active backdrop filter blur of `8px`. Prompts immediate, deliberate focus on sizing or pricing clarity.
- **Intelligence Focus:** Active copilot cards feature an ultra-thin inner glow via a dual box-shadow: `0 0 0 1px #1A1A1A, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a refined, architectural curvature. Elements use restrained corner radii that balance modern friendliness with editorial discipline.

- **Foundational Surfaces:** Standard cards, dialogs, and panels feature `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg` under roundedness `1`), preserving crisp, structural garment silhouettes without harsh right angles.
- **Micro UI & Interactive Controls:** Input fields, dropdown triggers, and buttons use `0.25rem` (`rounded` base) to project precision.
- **Pill Exception for Intelligence Tags:** Status pills, confidence tags, score indicators, and filter tokens use fully rounded pill geometries (`border-radius: 9999px`) to immediately separate qualitative evaluation metadata from rectangular layout containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Add to Bag with Fit Guarantee"):** Solid `#1A1A1A` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, `48px` minimum touch target height. Bold Outfit label (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#333333`.
- **Copilot Action (e.g., "Analyze Fit Uncertainty"):** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#1A1A1A` border, `#1A1A1A` typography. Subtle magenta tint `#FDF0F5` on hover with a `#E11B6C` hairline shift.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Pure `#1A1A1A` text with no container border, underlined on hover, used for secondary dismissals or sizing chart drill-downs.

### Decision Confidence Pills
- Formed as full pill shapes (`9999px`) containing a `6px` solid status circle dot alongside uppercase `label-sm` text.
- **Buy Now:** Green tint background (`#ECFDF5`), green text (`#059669`), green border (`#A7F3D0`).
- **Wait / Price Alert:** Amber tint background (`#FEF3C7`), amber text (`#D97706`), amber border (`#FDE68A`).
- **Fit Doubts:** Coral tint background (`#FEF2F2`), coral text (`#DC2626`), coral border (`#FECACA`).

### Readiness & Fit Progress Bars
- Linear horizontal score visualizers measuring `4px` in height against a `#E8E8E5` track with a `2px` radius.
- Dynamic fill color directly correlates with confidence level (Emerald for 85%+, Amber for 60-84%, Coral for <60%).
- Accompanied by numeric metric tags positioned top-right in tabular tabular Outfit numerals.

### Product Wishlist Cards
- Structured inside a `1px` border of `#E8E8E5` on `#FFFFFF` with `0.5rem` radius.
- Imagery dominates the top tier at a 3:4 aspect ratio with zero padding, subtly treated with neutral background fills.
- Bottom container contains the product brand (Outfit uppercase `label-sm`), garment name (Bodoni Moda `headline-sm`), price, and the embedded Copilot Decision Module cleanly separated by a fine `#E8E8E5` rule.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` `#E8E8E5`, `44px` height, `0.25rem` radius.
- Focus state eliminates standard browser rings and triggers a sharp `1px` solid `#1A1A1A` border with no displacement.
- Error states enforce `1px` solid `#DC2626` with red assistive label text beneath.

### Copilot Intelligence Insight Panel
- Collapsible inline card nested within items exhibiting fit or price volatility.
- Uses `#FBFBF9` surface fill with an `8px` left accent border matching the decision state (Emerald, Amber, or Coral).
- Features concise, natural language rationale: e.g., *"Returns on this cut are high for shoulders narrower than 38cm. We recommend sizing down to M based on your past orders."*
